*I know guys, I know.*

For some folks, the Neo•Geo is such an iconic symbol that even thinking of altering one of its games feels like sacrilege. Some others will think that lowering the games' difficulty is a disgrace, and that people need to "git gud".

The truth is: Neo•Geo games were, for the most part, absolutely not balanced towards player fun. Before anything else, the goal was to make them throw money. Yes, all of them can still be 1CC'ed, but this requires TONS of time, which is probably the most precious resource for every retrogamer.

To this day, if one wants to step into the Neo•Geo world, only two options exist:

1. Put a lot of time and effort into getting through the game, neglecting an already gigantic retro-backlog (Hi! It's me!).
2. Get through the game while credits-feeding it, or using invincibility cheats, which -imo- absolutely kills the fun and is the worst way to discover a game.

The majority of the hacks I'll make will be directed toward a **third option**: make all these fantastic games more accessible, while still maintaining a challenge. All the hacks have a custom in-game menu (also present in MVS mode) that lets you tweak the gameplay elements to your liking (except Magician Lord, but it will be done soon). By default, the game will always start with its original settings.

This way, anyone can have fun discovering these games, and it can also be good training before tackling the original difficulty.
