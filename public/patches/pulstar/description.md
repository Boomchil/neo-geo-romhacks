# Pulstar Aegis

Pulstar is one of the most impressive shmups ever made, but its brutal difficulty can make it hard to appreciate everything it has to offer.

This romhack will add a few quality-of-life features to let you survive longer, learn the game, and enjoy the ride without breaking your beloved sticks.
