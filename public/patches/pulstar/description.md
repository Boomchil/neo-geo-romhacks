# Pulstar Aegis

Pulstar is Aicom's 1995 answer to R-Type, and still one of the most jaw-dropping technical showcases on the Neo-Geo. But it has always been brutally unforgiving, so much so that its sequel, Blazing Star, allegedly changed its name from "Pulstar Blast" to avoid scaring players away.

The goal of this romhack is to add quality-of-life features without trivializing the game. It remains a solid challenge, while giving players the option to survive long enough to discover and enjoy it.

## List of Changes

### Gameplay

- **Armor system**: choose between 1, 2 or 3 armor points, which protect you from dying instantly when hit. The remaining armor is shown on the HUD, just above the lives.
- **Autofire**: two selectable modes:
    - *Semi-assisted:* keeps your firepower maxed with a relaxed tapping rhythm instead of frantic mashing.
    - *On:* the ship auto-fires while you hold [A], and charging moves to button [C].
- **Fast Charge**: doubles the charge speed.
- **Module Bomb button combination**: choose between [A+B] and [B+C]. Some players found the original combination triggered against their will; [B+C] should help avoid that.

### Others

- The How to Play screen shows the controls according to your settings.
- Small fix that reduces frame drops by ~15–20%. The game still lags a lot though... ^^'

## How to configure the game

### AES

Just open the **Config** menu on the title screen.

### MVS (Freeplay disabled)

After inserting a credit, the Compulsion Screen shows up for the delay set in the cabinet configuration. You can then press [D] to bring up the options menu.

> **Warning:** if GAME START COMPULSION is set to WITHOUT in SETTING UP THE CABINET, this screen won't show up. Either configure it, or see **MVS (Freeplay enabled)** below.

### MVS (Freeplay enabled)

With Freeplay enabled, the Compulsion Screen is skipped entirely. Simply wait until the end of the demo, when the Pulstar logo appears with the planet in the background, then press [D] to open the options menu.
