# Sengoku 2 - Let there be blood

Based on screenshots that were unfortunately never made available to the public, it appears that Sengoku 2 was originally intended to be an extremely violent game. Much of this is still visible today, particularly in the many dismemberment animations that were unfortunately "censored" by simply changing the color of the blood.

This romhack will restore the original blood color and adds the bodies that remain on the ground, as well as blood stains, along with a few quality-of-life options such as adjustable character speed, stage selection, and more. A separate patch without the blood and gore will also be available for those who prefer to keep the game's vanilla look while still enjoying the new features.
